# Tone: learn-and-grow-together (Remy-style)

Reference: aiwithremy.com. Warm, generous, peer-to-peer — a marketer learning AI in public, inviting readers along.

Rules:
- First person, "I'm figuring this out / here's what I tried / go build your own version".
- Always hand something over: try it, tweak it, take the idea, make yours.
- Hero: "Let's learn AI marketing together."
- Banned: guru/hype language, "10x", "do the work of five", "stolen", "field log", "dispatch", "operator" positioning, "zero fluff", agency-speak, AI-slop superlatives.
- Honest and modest: what worked AND what didn't. No claims of authority or mastery.
